Which fits?

01 · Field Operations
Maximum information density
Feels purposeful and serious
Easy to scan under pressure
Dark — harder on bright venues
Monospace can feel cold
Less familiar to non-tech users
02 · Warm Editorial
Feels premium and considered
Readable in any lighting
Reports feel like documents worth sharing
Serif type slower to scan quickly
Less "live ops" energy
Further from current tool feel
03 · Kinetic Card
Familiar — Buzz colours, same logic
Mobile-first card layout works well
Easiest migration from current tool
Avatars add warmth to the people lists
Most conventional of the three
Risk of feeling like a reskin not a rebuild
